Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy ("Policy") explains how this website, available at https://ampedsuccess.com/ ("Website"), uses cookies and similar technologies (collectively "Tools") provided either by us or by third parties.

These services are operated by Amped Success, a U.S.-based company ("Company," "we," "our," or "us"). The Company is the data controller for the data processing described in this Policy.

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your device. Comparable technologies include web storage (local/session storage), fingerprints, tags, or pixels. Most browsers are set by default to accept cookies and similar technologies. However, you can usually adjust your browser settings so that these Tools are rejected or only stored with your prior consent. If you refuse these Tools, you may not be able to use all of our services without issues.

Below, we list the Tools we use, the providers of these Tools, the storage duration, and any data transfers to third parties. We also explain when we obtain your voluntary consent to use the Tools and how you can revoke this consent.

1. Legal Basis and Revocation.

1.1 Legal Basis We use Tools necessary for operating the Website based on our legitimate interest in providing you with a seamless and efficient browsing experience. Some Tools may also be necessary for performing a contract or carrying out pre-contractual measures.

For all other Tools, including marketing and analytics, we rely on your consent. If personal data is transferred to third countries, we refer you to our Privacy Policy, which outlines potential risks. If you provide consent for specific Tools, data processed using these Tools may also be transferred based on your consent.

1.2 Obtaining Your Consent Our Website uses Hubspot to collect and manage consent and revocations. When you select an option in the consent banner, a cookie is set to record your preference. This is a technically necessary cookie that we use based on our legitimate interest in documenting your consent.

If you delete your cookies, we will ask for your consent again when you next access the Website.

1.3 Withdrawing Your Consent or Changing Your Selection You can withdraw consent for specific or all Tools at any time. To do so, please email info@ampedsuccess.com. You can also adjust your preferences directly through your browser settings or, where applicable, revoke consent directly with the provider.

2. Essential Cookies. Essential cookies enable the fundamental functionality of our Website. These Tools do not require consent and are used based on our legitimate interest in ensuring the operation of our Website.

2.1 Hubspot Our Website uses Hubspot, a service provided by Hubspot Ireland Limited, 2nd Floor, 30 North Wall Quay, Dublin 1, Ireland, for consent management and form functionality.

Cookies used:

  • _hs_cookie_cat_pref (6 months): Records categories of tools a visitor has consented to.
  • __cfuvid (session): Applied by HubSpot’s CDN provider for rate-limiting purposes.

2.1 Other Essential Tools

  • JSESSIONID (session): Manages user sessions.
3. Non-Essential Cookies. We use Tools for analytics and marketing purposes based on your consent. These Tools help us understand user behavior and improve our Website.

3.1 Hubspot Analytics We use Hubspot to track user interactions on our Website.

Cookies used:

  • __hstc (6 months): Tracks user visits and session activity.
  • hubspotutk (6 months): Identifies returning visitors.
  • __hssc (30 minutes): Manages session tracking.
  • __hssrc (session): Identifies new sessions.

For more details, refer to Hubspot’s Privacy Policy.

4. Changes to This Cookie Policy. We may update this Policy periodically. Any changes take effect when posted on this page. We encourage users to review this Policy regularly.
