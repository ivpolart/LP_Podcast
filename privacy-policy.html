Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy ("Privacy Policy") explains how we process and protect your personal data when you use this website or our services provided via https://ampedsuccess.com/ (together, the "Services").

These Services are operated by Amped Success, a U.S.-based company ("Company," "we," "our," or "us"). The Company is the data controller for the processing described below.

Unless otherwise defined in this Privacy Policy, the definitions used align with applicable U.S. data protection laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and other relevant regulations.

1. Personal Data We Collect. We may collect or receive personal information for various purposes connected with our business operations when you use our Services, including:
  • Usage and analytics information (e.g., IP address, browsing history, tracking data)
  • Contact details (e.g., name, email, phone number, mailing address)
  • Payment details (e.g., billing information, credit card details)
  • Request details (e.g., inquiries, messages, or feedback)
  • Website visitor details (e.g., device information, log files)

You are not obligated to provide your personal data, but failure to do so may prevent us from offering you certain services.

2. How We Collect Personal Data. We collect personal data through the following methods:

Directly:

  • When you use our website or services
  • When you provide services to us
  • When you communicate with us electronically
  • When you submit forms or inquiries

Indirectly:

  • From publicly available sources (e.g., social media, public records)
  • When third-party services, such as analytics providers, share data with us
3. Legal Basis and Purposes. We process your personal data based on:
  • Contractual necessity: To provide our Services, including customer support and recruitment.
  • Consent: For marketing communications and non-essential cookies.
  • Legitimate interests: To improve our Services and ensure security.
  • Legal compliance: To meet legal obligations and enforce our rights.
4. Data Retention. We retain personal data as long as necessary for our business and legal obligations. Once no longer needed, we delete or anonymize the data.
5. Data Sharing and Recipients. We may share your personal data with:
  • Professional advisors (e.g., accountants, lawyers)
  • IT and software service providers
  • Third-party payment processors
  • Marketing and recruitment partners
  • Regulatory authorities when required by law

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

6. Data Transfers. We process personal data primarily in the U.S. If data is transferred outside the U.S., we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.
7. Data Security. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect personal data. However, no method of data transmission is completely secure. We encourage users to take precautions, such as using antivirus software.
8. Your Rights. Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:
  • Right to access: Request a copy of your data.
  • Right to correction: Ask us to update or correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to deletion: Request data deletion under certain conditions.
  • Right to object to processing: Limit certain data processing activities.
  • Right to opt out: If applicable under CCPA, opt out of data sales or certain sharing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@ampedsuccess.com.

9. Third-Party Links and Social Media. Our Services may contain links to third-party websites or social media platforms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before interacting with them.
10. Changes to This Privacy Policy. We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes take effect when posted on this page. We encourage users to review it regularly.
