Why Work with Matt?

With over 26 years of experience in business acquisition and investments, Matt Uhler is a trusted expert dedicated to guiding business owners and investors to success. Having evaluated more than 2,500 deals and successfully closed over 800 transactions—totaling over $250 million in funded deals—Matt has developed a deep understanding of the complexities involved in buying, selling, and financing businesses. His approach is grounded in strategic decision-making and operational efficiency, empowering his clients to navigate the intricate world of business investments with clarity and confidence.

Matt’s passion is educating individuals on the powerful advantages of acquiring established businesses rather than starting from scratch. His expertise spans topics such as business acquisition, roll-up strategies, partnership development, and creative investing. Whether working with aspiring entrepreneurs or seasoned investors, Matt emphasizes how small business acquisition provides a proven pathway to financial freedom by leveraging existing operations, customer bases, and market presence.

Through Amped Success, Matt brings his wealth of knowledge to entrepreneurs and investors eager to make sound financial decisions and grow wealth through strategic opportunities.
